The academy

About the academy

JV Ice Hockey Academy was established in London in 2014 to provide structured ice hockey coaching with a strong emphasis on technical development.

Our academy helps players build skating fundamentals, improve individual hockey skills and develop confidence through purposeful training.

We believe every player should have the opportunity to progress within a supportive, challenging and enjoyable hockey environment.

How we coach

Coaching philosophy

01

Skating first

Strong skating technique is essential to hockey development.

02

Individual progression

Every player develops at a different pace.

03

Purposeful practice

Structured repetition and effective technical instruction help build consistent skills.

04

Confidence & enjoyment

We encourage players to develop confidence, work hard and enjoy hockey.
