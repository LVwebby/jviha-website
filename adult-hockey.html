Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for adult players. Can't find yours? Ask us.

Can I join as a complete beginner?

Yes. Our Tuesday sessions welcome adults who are new to ice hockey or still developing their skating and hockey skills, in a supportive environment.

What is the difference between Tuesday and Friday hockey?

Tuesday is for beginners and players still developing their skills. Friday is for players who already have established skating and hockey experience, with a more experienced playing environment.

Where are adult sessions held?

At Queens Ice Rink, 17 Queensway, Bayswater, London W2 4QP.

How do I join and pay?

Adult hockey is managed through Spond, separately from our children's programmes. Spond is where you'll find confirmed session dates, registration, monthly subscriptions, payments and updates.

What equipment do I need?

Full ice hockey equipment is recommended. As a minimum: a helmet with cage, neck guard, knee or shin pads, elbow pads, gloves, skates and a hockey stick. Equipment rental may be available at the rink, subject to availability, so ask us before your first session.

I'm not sure which session suits me. What should I do?

Message us on WhatsApp or use our contact form and we'll help you choose.
