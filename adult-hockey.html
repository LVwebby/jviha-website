It's never too late to play

JV Ice Hockey Academy offers regular adult hockey sessions for people looking to learn, improve their skating and hockey abilities, or enjoy organised ice hockey with other players.

Our sessions cater to different levels of experience.

Tuesday sessions welcome adults who are new to ice hockey or still developing their skating and hockey skills.
