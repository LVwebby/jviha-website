Can Adults Learn Ice Hockey? Starting From Zero in London

23 November 2026 · JV Ice Hockey Academy

Yes, adults can absolutely learn ice hockey, even starting from zero. JV Ice Hockey Academy runs regular adult sessions at Queens Ice Rink in Bayswater, central London, for people learning the game, improving their skating, or simply enjoying organised hockey with other players.

Is it too late to start ice hockey as an adult?

It's never too late. Many adults take up hockey after watching a game, joining a friend or wanting a new way to keep fit. You don't need a background in the sport. What you need is a willingness to learn to skate properly, because skating is the foundation of everything else in hockey.

Two sessions for different levels

Our adult hockey runs on two evenings, so you can play at a level that suits you:

  • Tuesday hockey (beginners welcome): for adults who are new to ice hockey or still developing their skating and hockey skills. It's a supportive environment to gain experience, build confidence and improve.
  • Friday hockey (intermediate and advanced): for players who already have established skating and hockey experience, with a more experienced playing environment.

Not sure which suits you? Send us a message and we'll help you choose.

What will I learn?

As a beginner, expect to focus on skating first: balance, edges, stopping and turning. Then come stick handling, passing and shooting, and finally playing as part of a team. Progress comes quickly once your skating feels comfortable.

What equipment do adults need?

Full ice hockey equipment is recommended. As a minimum, you'll need a helmet with cage, neck guard, knee or shin pads, elbow pads, gloves, skates and a hockey stick. Equipment rental may be available at the rink, subject to availability. Ask us before your first session.

How to join

Adult hockey is managed through Spond, separately from our children's programmes. Spond is where you'll find confirmed session dates, registration, monthly subscriptions, payments and updates. Register through Spond, or message us first if you have questions.

Frequently asked questions

Can adults learn ice hockey from scratch?

Yes. JV Ice Hockey Academy in London welcomes adult beginners on Tuesdays, for people new to ice hockey or still developing their skating and hockey skills.

When is adult ice hockey in London?

JV Ice Hockey Academy runs adult hockey on Tuesdays (beginners welcome) and Fridays (intermediate and advanced) at Queens Ice Rink, Bayswater.

Do I need my own equipment?

Full equipment is recommended, with a minimum of helmet with cage, neck guard, knee or shin pads, elbow pads, gloves, skates and stick. Rental may be available at the rink, subject to availability.
