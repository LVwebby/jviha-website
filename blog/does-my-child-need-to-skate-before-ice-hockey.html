Does My Child Need to Skate Before Starting Ice Hockey?

23 November 2026 · JV Ice Hockey Academy

No, your child doesn't need to be a skater before starting ice hockey. At JV Ice Hockey Academy in London, complete beginners are welcome, and skating is exactly where we start, because strong skating is the foundation of every good hockey player.

Can complete beginners join?

Yes. For younger players, previous skating experience isn't necessarily required, although being comfortable moving independently on the ice is strongly recommended. Complete beginners are usually directed towards a suitable Sunday session, and our team reviews each player's age and experience before final group placement.

Why we put skating first

In hockey, everything happens on skates: chasing the puck, passing, shooting and staying balanced in a battle. A player who skates well has more time, more confidence and more fun. That's why our coaching builds skating fundamentals before anything else.

Skating skills we develop

  • Forward and backward skating
  • Balance and edge control
  • Acceleration and stopping
  • Turns and transitions
  • Agility and skating efficiency

Alongside skating, players develop puck handling, passing, shooting, hockey awareness and decision-making.

Should we take public skating lessons first?

It can help, but it isn't required. Time on the ice during public skating builds comfort and confidence, so if your family enjoys it, go for it. When you're ready for hockey, our Learn to Skate and beginner pathway takes it from there.

How to start

Register through Spond and our team will confirm the most suitable session for your child. Not sure? Ask us.

Frequently asked questions

Does my child need to know how to skate before ice hockey?

No. Complete beginners are welcome at JV Ice Hockey Academy. Being comfortable moving independently on the ice is strongly recommended, and skating is the first thing we teach.

Where do complete beginners start?

Complete beginners are usually directed towards a suitable Sunday session. Our team reviews each player's age and experience before final group placement.

What skating skills will my child learn?

Forward and backward skating, balance and edge control, acceleration and stopping, turns and transitions, and agility.
