Ice Hockey for Kids in London: A Parent's Complete Guide

23 November 2026 · JV Ice Hockey Academy

Thinking about ice hockey for your child in London? This guide covers how children's hockey works at JV Ice Hockey Academy, from the first time on the ice to playing in an age group, so you know exactly what to expect.

Where can children play ice hockey in central London?

JV Ice Hockey Academy trains at Queens Ice Rink, 17 Queensway, Bayswater, London W2 4QP. We have been developing young hockey players in London since 2014, under founder and head coach Vid Jazbec, a former professional player.

Why we start with skating

Strong skating is the foundation of every good hockey player. That's why our sessions focus on forward and backward skating, balance and edge control, stopping, turning and agility, alongside puck handling, passing, shooting and hockey awareness. Read more in Does my child need to skate before starting ice hockey?

Age groups

Children's groups run from U7 Learn to Skate to U18 Development. Players are placed by age, skating ability, hockey experience and overall development, and our team confirms the final group after registration. See the current groups on our children's hockey page.

When are sessions?

Children's academy sessions generally take place during school terms on:

  • Tuesday after school
  • Saturday morning
  • Sunday morning

Confirmed times for each group are shared through Spond once your registration is approved.

What equipment does my child need?

Full ice hockey equipment is recommended. As a minimum, players need a helmet with cage, neck guard, knee or shin pads, elbow pads, gloves, skates and a hockey stick. Rental may be available at the rink, subject to availability. See the equipment list on our children's hockey page.

How registration works

  • Register through Spond: a parent or guardian completes the form, declarations and consent choices.
  • We review your request: we confirm the right group by age, ability and availability.
  • Get on the ice: once approved, you'll find session times and booking options in Spond.

Registering doesn't guarantee an immediate place, as not every group may be accepting new players at all times. We'll let you know about availability.

Safety and wellbeing

Medical information is collected securely through Spond during registration, never through the website. Parents watch from behind the glass or in the spectator area. Our Child Protection Officer is Julija Ĺ tefe, and our safeguarding information is on the Policies page.

Frequently asked questions

Where can my child play ice hockey in central London?

JV Ice Hockey Academy runs children's ice hockey at Queens Ice Rink, 17 Queensway, Bayswater, London W2 4QP, and has done since 2014.

What days are children's ice hockey sessions?

Sessions generally take place during school terms on Tuesday after school and Saturday and Sunday mornings. Group times are shared through Spond after approval.

How do I register my child?

Register through Spond. Our team reviews each registration and confirms the right group based on age, ability, experience and availability.
