What hockey equipment does my child need?

23 November 2026 · JV Ice Hockey Academy

If your child is starting ice hockey, the list of kit can look long. Here's what they need for academy sessions, and how to sort out rental if you're not ready to buy everything yet.

The minimum equipment

We encourage all players to wear full ice hockey equipment. As a minimum, every player needs:

  • Helmet with cage to protect the head and face
  • Neck guard
  • Knee or shin pads
  • Elbow pads
  • Gloves
  • Skates
  • Hockey stick

Can we rent equipment?

Equipment rental may be available at the rink, subject to availability. If your child needs rental equipment, please contact us in advance and let us know:

  • your child's approximate height
  • their exact skate size

Players who need fitting should arrive at least 30 minutes before their session, so there's time to get everything on properly before they step onto the ice.

Any questions?

If you're unsure about anything, from sizing to what to bring on the first day, message us and we'll be happy to help.
