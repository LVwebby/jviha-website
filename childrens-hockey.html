Our approach

Skating fundamentals first

At JV Ice Hockey Academy, we believe that strong skating fundamentals provide the foundation for long-term hockey development.

Our children's programmes are designed to help young players improve their skating, develop hockey skills, build confidence and enjoy being on the ice.

We welcome players with different levels of experience, from complete beginners to established youth players. Players are placed into suitable groups based on age, skating ability, hockey experience and overall development.

New to the ice?

Learn to Skate & beginners

Complete beginners are welcome.

For younger players, previous skating experience isn't necessarily required, although being comfortable moving independently on the ice is strongly recommended.

Complete beginners are usually directed towards a suitable Sunday session. Our team reviews each player's age and experience before final group placement.

On the ice

Skills we develop

  • Forward & backward skating
  • Balance & edge control
  • Acceleration & stopping
  • Turns & transitions
  • Agility & skating efficiency
  • Puck handling & control
  • Passing & receiving
  • Shooting technique
  • Hockey awareness
  • Decision-making
  • Individual technical development

Kit

Equipment

Players are encouraged to wear full ice hockey equipment.

Equipment rental may be available at the rink, subject to availability. If you need rental equipment, please contact us in advance with your child's approximate height and exact skate size. Players who need fitting should arrive at least 30 minutes early.

Read our equipment guide

Minimum equipment

  • Helmet with cage
  • Neck guard
  • Knee or shin pads
  • Elbow pads
  • Gloves
  • Skates
  • Hockey stick
