This website and the enquiry form

Our Privacy Notice explains how JV Ice Hockey Academy LTD handles personal information. This section adds how this website, jvhockey.com, works.

  • Enquiry form: when you send an enquiry, we receive the details you enter (enquiry type, your name, email, and optionally your phone number and the player's age, plus your message). The form is delivered to info@jvhockey.com by Web3Forms, a form delivery service. We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and keep a record of our communication.
  • Please don't send sensitive information through the form. Medical and consent information is collected securely through Spond during registration.
  • Hosting: the website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and to keep the service running.
  • Fonts: the website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Cookies: we only use Google Analytics if you accept it in the cookie banner. We don't use advertising cookies. See the Cookie Policy below.
  • Other services: links to Spond, WhatsApp and Google Maps open those services, which have their own privacy policies.

To ask about your information or use your data protection rights, email info@jvhockey.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files saved by your browser. We use one essential cookie, and Google Analytics cookies only if you accept them.

  • jv_consent (essential, 6 months): remembers your cookie choice.
  • _ga (analytics, Google Analytics, up to 2 years): distinguishes visitors so we can count visits anonymously.
  • _ga_<ID> (analytics, Google Analytics, up to 2 years): keeps track of a visit session.

You can change your choice at any time: open cookie settings. Rejecting analytics removes the Google Analytics cookies.

Safeguarding & Child Protection

The academy's Child Protection Officer is Julija Štefe, Academy Administrator. Safeguarding questions or concerns can be raised with her by email at info@jvhockey.com.

Any concern about a child's safety, welfare or wellbeing should be raised with an academy administrator, coach or appropriate safeguarding contact as soon as possible.

JV Ice Hockey Academy has a zero tolerance approach to verbal abuse, physical abuse, bullying, discrimination, intimidation, threats, unsafe behaviour and serious disrespect. This applies in person, online and through messaging platforms.

Players are encouraged to tell a coach or trusted adult if they feel unsafe, worried, bullied or uncomfortable.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 999.

Cancellation Policy

Once a booking has been confirmed, payments are non refundable unless JV Ice Hockey Academy cancels the session.

If a player cannot attend a booked session, JV Ice Hockey Academy is unable to guarantee a refund or replacement session. Where possible, and at JV Ice Hockey Academy's discretion, credit may be offered towards a future session or term.

If JV Ice Hockey Academy has to cancel a session due to rink availability, coach availability, safety concerns, or another reason outside normal planning, families will be offered either a replacement session or credit towards a future booking.

Missed sessions due to personal reasons, illness, holidays, lateness, or personal schedule conflicts are not automatically refundable.

All credits must be used within the time period confirmed by JV Ice Hockey Academy and cannot be exchanged for cash.
